LINKD Wireframes

Agreed layouts for the app's screens, drawn with the Pink drink tokens from the style guide. Mid-fi: real structure and copy, placeholder icons, no final art. Behavior belongs to the SOS alert flow; this page only shows where things sit.

  • SOS core
  • Emergency contacts
  • Sign-in and onboarding
  • Rules and open items

SOS core

Home, the two countdowns, the active alert, and the unprotected warning. The countdown is the most important screen in the app: full screen, one action, nothing to navigate.

Emergency contacts

Up to five people who are always texted. A contact who replies STOP stays listed as opted out and no longer counts toward the five.

Sign-in and onboarding

Email and password, then the profile contacts will see, each permission on its own explained screen, and the dispatch choice.

Rules every screen follows

From the style guide and the mobile protocol. A wireframe that breaks one of these is wrong, not stylish.

  • SOS is red, labelled, and has an iconPink and red are the same lightness, so SOS never relies on hue. Pink is for everyday actions only.
  • Cancel is at least 88ptFull width, white on midnight, reachable one-handed.
  • Words carry every stateConnected, Texted, Failed, Opted out: a badge always has text.
  • No silent failureIf a text did not send, the screen says so and offers the fallback.
  • System font for body and SOSNothing on an emergency screen can fail to load. The display font is for headings.
  • Discreet on the lock screenNotifications do not reveal SOS details unless the user opts in.
